The wedding of

John & Christina

September 4, 2027 · Snogebæk, Bornholm

John and Christina met in 2019 at Johns Hopkins in Baltimore. Both were enrolled in Johns Hopkins’ Astronomy and Astrophysics PhD program. Through classes, homework study sessions, and weekly happy hours, they became friends and then quickly fell in love. Despite a pandemic, internships, travel, and the infamous academic two-body problem, Christina and John have grown closer together. They see this ceremony and party as an opportunity to celebrate a milestone in their relationship with their closest friends and family.
